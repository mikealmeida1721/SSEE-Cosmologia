To the Editors of *Physical Review D*,

I am pleased to submit for your consideration the manuscript **"Fixing the
Dark-Energy Equation of State with Two Dimensionless Constants, φ and π: A
Falsifiable, Parameter-Frugal Test Against DESI DR2 and Planck 2018."**

**Physics claim.** The manuscript addresses a question made pressing by the DESI
results: whether the dark-energy equation of state is a set of free parameters to
be measured, or a structure fixed in advance. It presents a minimal-parameter
framework in which the equation-of-state pair follows as bare algebraic ratios of
two dimensionless constants, w0 = −0.840 and wa = −0.670, with no fitted amplitude.
This pre-registered point is confronted with DESI DR2 (0.24σ, Pantheon+) and Planck
2018 (PR3 spectra, PR4 lensing), and carries a pre-committed DESI DR3 falsification criterion (joint exclusion
at >3σ refutes the model). Where standard extensions add parameters to fit the data,
this framework removes them while remaining consistent with BAO, CMB, and
structure-growth observations. I believe this sharply falsifiable, parameter-frugal
proposal is of direct interest to the PRD readership working on dynamical dark
energy and the Hubble tension.

**Originality and status.** This manuscript is original, has not been published
previously, and is not under consideration at any other journal. A preprint of the
associated framework is permanently archived on Zenodo (concept DOI
10.5281/zenodo.19654118); the work has **not** been posted to arXiv and has **not**
been previously submitted to or rejected by any journal.

**Author.** I am an independent researcher without institutional affiliation; I have
noted this in the submission comments to the editors. All code, data, and validation
scripts are publicly available and reproducible, as stated in the manuscript's Data
and Code Availability section.

**Suggested referees (optional).** Researchers active in dynamical dark energy and
the w0–wa parametrization would be well placed to assess this work; I am happy to
provide specific names on request.
