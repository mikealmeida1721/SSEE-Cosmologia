We present a detailed analysis of redshift-space distortions in the two-point correlation function of the 6dF Galaxy Survey (6dFGS). The $K$-band selected sub-sample which we employ in this study contains $81\,971$ galaxies distributed over $17\,000\,$deg$^2$ with an effective redshift $z_{\rm eff} = 0.067$. By modelling the 2D galaxy correlation function, $\xi(r_p,\pi)$, we measure the parameter combination $f(z_{\rm eff})\sigma_8(z_{\rm eff}) = 0.423 \pm 0.055$, where $f \simeq \Omega_m^{\gamma}(z)$ is the growth rate of cosmic structure and $\sigma_8$ is the r.m.s. of matter fluctuations in $8h^{-1}\,$Mpc spheres.\\
