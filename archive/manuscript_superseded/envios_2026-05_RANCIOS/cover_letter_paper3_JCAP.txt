SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

A persistent challenge for minimal-parameter dark energy models is
compatibility with the CMB acoustic scale, which encodes Ω_m at recombination.
The SSEE framework predicts Ω_m,dyn = 0.160, which naively shifts the first
acoustic peak from ℓ = 220 to ℓ = 242 — which would apparently falsify the
model. This paper shows that the CMB matter density is, instead, an
independent algebraic prediction of the framework.

THE ALGEBRAIC CMB MATTER DENSITY

The SSEE CMB matter density is fixed algebraically, not rescaled from the
dynamic sector. It is the standard physical observable
ω_m = ω_b + ω_c + ω_ν, with each piece given by φ and π:
ω_b = (π − φ)/(3Ω²) = 0.02242 (Ω = π + φ), ω_c = KAL₀·ω_b·n_s = 0.11951
(a forward identity, 0.41σ from Planck, with KAL₀ = β + π and n_s = 1 − φ⁻⁷),
and ω_ν = Σm_ν/93.14 = 0.000741. This gives Ω_m,CMB = ω_m/h² = 0.30889 at the
algebraic background H₀ = 3(φ+π)² = 67.962 km/s/Mpc, within 1σ of Planck 2018
(0.3153 ± 0.0073). Crucially, Ω_m,dyn (DESI) and Ω_m,CMB (Planck) are two
independent predictions of SSEE — there is no matter-rescaling factor linking
them (the earlier "MIRA-mapping" of this manuscript has been retired; OP-8).

MAIN RESULTS

With the algebraically-fixed background Ω_m,CMB = 0.30889:

  - TT power spectrum: χ²_r = 1.042 vs ΛCDM's 1.043 (N = 1971 multipoles)
  - TE polarisation: χ²_r = 1.040 vs 1.040 (N = 1967) — essentially identical
  - EE polarisation: χ²_r = 1.040 vs 1.039 (N = 1967) — essentially identical
  - CMB lensing (9 MV bandpowers): χ²_r = 0.719 vs 0.757 — SSEE marginally
    favoured
  - Full Planck plik MCMC (k=2 SSEE vs k=6 ΛCDM, N = 2354): best-fit
    χ² = 2771.3 vs 2773.1 — indistinguishable fits — ΔBIC = −32.9 favouring
    SSEE by parsimony (cross-checked at ΔBIC = −23.9 on plik_lite TTTEEE)

SSEE-V3.6 is not falsified by any of the four CMB observables tested.
Three explicit falsifiability criteria are provided in §6.

RELATION TO COMPANION PAPERS

This is Paper 3 of a four-paper series. Papers 1 and 2 are submitted
concurrently; Paper 4 extends the algebraic derivations to all CMB background
observables. Paper 2 provides the MCMC validation of the dynamical sector
against DESI DR2 and galaxy clusters. The present paper closes the CMB sector.

We note that the ΔBIC comparison presented in §5 uses k=2 parameters for SSEE
({A_s, τ}; the remaining four ΛCDM parameters are fixed algebraically) against
k=6 for ΛCDM, making the ΔBIC = −32.9 result (full plik; −23.9 via plik_lite)
a parsimony-driven test in SSEE's favour at statistically indistinguishable
fit quality. The companion Paper 2 reports a separate ΔBIC = +206 for the full
background sector under ΛCDM-calibrated Friedmann priors; these two results
address distinct physical questions and should not be conflated.
