SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

The DESI DR2 baryon acoustic oscillation data (Abdul-Karim et al. 2025)
provide the most precise current measurement of the dark energy equation of
state, with a preference for dynamical dark energy at approximately 3σ.
This situation motivates systematic testing of minimal-parameter dark energy
models that predict (w₀, wₐ) algebraically.

The Structural Self-Energy Expansion (SSEE-V3.6) framework derives the dark
energy equation of state from the golden ratio φ and π alone:
w₀ = -Tᵣ/Mᵥ = -0.840 and wₐ = -P_sc/Kᵥ = -0.670, with no parameter fitting.
These values are fixed by a closed algebraic dictionary of π and the golden
ratio φ (archived at Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.20684908) with no fitting to
the data. The agreement with the DESI DR2 dynamical-dark-energy trend is
therefore a parameter-free postdiction; its strength lies in the structural
rigidity of the closed dictionary, not in temporal priority. A genuinely
pre-registered test is reserved for the not-yet-public DESI DR3 and Euclid data.

MAIN RESULTS

Using emcee with N_walkers=100, N_steps=25000, and the full DESI DR2 13×13
BAO covariance matrix, we find:

  - H₀ = 67.95 ± 0.40 km/s/Mpc (SSEE posterior; algebraic background
    prior H₀ = 3(φ+π)² = 67.962, lowered by DESI BAO)
  - The algebraic prediction (w₀, wₐ) = (-0.840, -0.670) lies within 0.24σ
    (DESI+CMB+Pantheon+; 0.2–1.8σ across SN compilations)
    of the DESI DR2 CPL free-fit posterior (N_eff = 637,500 samples)
  - Galaxy-cluster mass ratios reproduced at χ²_r = 0.122 (4-cluster MCMC subset)
    and χ²_r = 0.126 (7-cluster analytic), using IGIMF-corrected baryonic masses,
    without cold dark matter
  - Dynamic sector isolated: ΔBIC(SSEE_dyn) = -5.55 vs ΛCDM (k=1 vs k=3),
    confirming the w₀-wₐ prediction is genuinely favoured
  - Full model: ΔBIC = +206 under standard Friedmann assumptions (k=0 vs k=6),
    arising from the enlarged sound horizon (r_d = 175.6 Mpc vs 147.6 Mpc);
    these are two distinct physical questions and must not be conflated

We note that the ΔBIC = +206 background penalty reflects the strict application
of ΛCDM-calibrated Friedmann priors to the SSEE background, not a failure of
the dynamical prediction. As demonstrated in the companion Paper 3, the CMB
matter density is itself an independent algebraic prediction —
ω_m = ω_b + ω_c + ω_ν with ω_c = KAL₀·ω_b·n_s — giving Ω_m,CMB = ω_m/h² =
0.30889 with no matter-rescaling factor; the acoustic-scale fit is then
statistically indistinguishable from ΛCDM (ΔBIC = −32.9 favouring SSEE by
parsimony), making the framework physically competitive with the standard
Friedmann model.
