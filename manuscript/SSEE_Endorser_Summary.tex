\subsection*{Core idea in one sentence}

The core cosmological background — $w_0$, $w_a$, $H_0$, $\Omega_m$, $n_s$ — is derived \emph{algebraically} from the
golden ratio $\varphi=(1+\sqrt{5})/2$ and $\pi$ with \textbf{zero fitted dimensionless parameters at the background level}. The kineticity $\alpha_K=\val{alpha_K_z0_d6}$ of the dark-energy condensate (Paper~7) is
derived, not fitted, and is unobservable on linear scales. The dark-sector ans\"atze that previously appeared here ($m_\varphi$, $\xi$) were retracted in 2026-08 together with the second matter sector, and their register entries (OP-9 through OP-12) close by dissolution; the MIRA matter mechanism (OP-8) had already dissolved under the $\omega_m$-direct reframe. The remaining open entries are listed in \texttt{OPEN\_PROBLEMS.md}.

\subsection*{Falsifiability (explicit)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $S_8=\val{S8_leg_pred}$ — with the background fixed by the algebra \emph{and}
    $A_s$ fixed to the value the CMB gives under that same background, the
    growth sector has no free cosmological parameter left: cosmic shear
    placing $S_8$ more than $3\sigma$ away falsifies it outright, with nowhere
    to absorb the discrepancy (KiDS, DES, Euclid).
  \item $\log(10^{10}A_s)$ — the amplitude the shear asks for must keep
    agreeing with the one the CMB fixes. KiDS-1000 asked for $\val{logA_k1000}\pm\val{logA_k1000_sigma}$
    ($\val{logA_k1000_vs_cmb}\sigma$ away) and KiDS-Legacy for $3.0255\pm0.0396$ ($0.49\sigma$);
    the two releases differ from each other by $\val{logA_entre_entregas}\sigma$. A future release
    returning to the lower value reopens the sector.
  \item $\alpha_T=\alpha_M=\alpha_B=0$ (Paper~7) — gravity is unmodified, $G_{\rm eff}=G_N$ and no gravitational slip; a detected slip or $\mu\neq1$ in Euclid/LSST lensing$\times$clustering would falsify the sector.
  \item $r=\varphi^{-10}=0.008131$ — LiteBIRD ($\sim$2032) will confirm or refute the tensor-to-scalar ratio.
  \item A $>3\sigma$ deviation of $w_0$ from $-0.840$ would falsify the corresponding background sector of the framework.
\end{itemize}
