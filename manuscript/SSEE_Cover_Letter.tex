\begin{letter}{The Editors\\Physical Review D}

\opening{Dear Editors,}

I am pleased to submit for your consideration the manuscript \textit{``Fixing
the Dark-Energy Equation of State with Two Dimensionless Constants, $\varphi$
and $\pi$: A Falsifiable, Parameter-Frugal Test Against DESI~DR2 and
Planck~2018.''}

\textbf{Physics claim.} The DESI results have made it pressing to ask whether
the dark-energy equation of state is a set of free parameters to be measured
or a structure fixed in advance. The manuscript presents a minimal-parameter
framework in which the pair $(w_0,w_a)$ follows as bare algebraic ratios of two
dimensionless constants, $w_0=\val{W0_d6}$ and $w_a=\val{WA_d6}$, with no fitted
amplitude. No quantity is fitted to place it, and it sits at
$\val{p2_w0wa_ssee_ec26}\sigma$ from its $w_0w_a$CDM posterior (Pantheon+;
$0.2$--$1.8\sigma$ across supernova compilations). It carries a pre-committed
falsification criterion: exclusion at $>3\sigma$ by DESI~DR3 refutes the model,
as would LiteBIRD failing to find $r=\varphi^{-10}=\val{r_tensor_mil_d6}\times10^{-3}$.

\textbf{One fit, then fixed.} In CMB-likelihood terms the framework is a $k=2$
model: the background ($\omega_b$, $\omega_c$, $n_s$, $w_0$, $w_a$ and the
global rate $H_0^{\rm glob}=H_{\rm SH0ES}(1-f_{\rm screen})=\val{H0_GLOBAL_d3}\pm\val{p9_hglob_sigma}~\kms$)
is fixed algebraically, and only the primordial amplitude $A_s$ and the optical
depth $\tau$ are fitted, once, to Planck~2018. The full \texttt{plik} likelihood
gives a best-fit $\chi^2$ indistinguishable from $\Lambda$CDM
($\Delta\chi^2=\val{b1_dchi2}$) and $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=\val{b1_dBIC}$ ($k=2$ versus
$k=6$): the preference comes from parsimony, not from a better fit. After that
single fit nothing cosmological is adjusted again. Every other probe is read
with the background of both models fixed in advance---SSEE at its algebraic
point, $\Lambda$CDM at the Planck best fit---and with no cosmological parameter
free. Supernovae, CMB lensing and weak lensing do not separate the two models;
the one probe that does is DESI~DR2 BAO, with $\chi^2=\val{desi_clav_ssee}$ for
SSEE and $\val{desi_clav_lcdm}$ for $\Lambda$CDM over $\val{desi_N}$ points.

\textbf{Weak lensing without an extra component.} An earlier version of this
work introduced an additional dark-matter component to relieve an apparent
$S_8$ tension. Measured against the raw KiDS-Legacy cosmic-shear data
(Wright et al.\ 2025, arXiv:2503.19441), that tension does not exist: with the
algebraic background, the shear asks for
$\log(10^{10}A_s)=\val{leg_logA}\pm\val{leg_logA_s}$, at $\val{logA_leg_vs_cmb}\sigma$
from the $\val{leg_logA_cmb}$ fixed by the model's own CMB fit. Fixing $A_s$ to that
value costs nothing ($\chi^2=\val{chi2_leg_unif}$ against $\val{chi2_leg_libre}$
with $A_s$ free, all remaining parameters nuisance), so
$S_8=\val{S8_leg_pred}$ is a prediction rather than a fit, against
$\val{S8_legacy_dato}\pm\val{S8_legacy_dato_sigma}$ measured. The additional
component was therefore withdrawn, and a single $A_s$ now serves the CMB and the
growth of structure. KiDS-Legacy predates this analysis; I claim consistency
across epochs, not priority.

\textbf{Limits stated in the manuscript.} The absolute vacuum scale enters as a
single measured input; the algebraic origin of $\omega_b$ and $\omega_c$ and the
mechanism behind the screening ratio are open problems, listed as such; the
non-linear closure of the growth amplitude is deferred to N-body work.

\textbf{Originality and status.} The manuscript is original, has not been
published elsewhere, and is not under consideration at any other journal. A
preprint of the associated framework is archived on Zenodo (concept DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19654118}{10.5281/zenodo.19654118}); it has
not been posted to arXiv. I am an independent researcher without institutional
affiliation and have no competing interests. All code, data provenance and
validation scripts are public
(\url{https://github.com/mikealmeida1721/SSEE-Cosmologia}), as stated in the
manuscript's Data and Code Availability section.

Thank you for considering this submission. I would be glad to answer any
